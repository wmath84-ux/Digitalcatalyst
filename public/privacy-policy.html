Privacy Policy

How Eduvora collects, uses, stores and protects your information — and exactly how we handle your Google data.

Last updated: 16 August 2026 Applies to: eduvora.shop

01

Introduction

Eduvora, operated by Digital Catalyst (“we”, “our” or “us”), is a digital learning service that offers online courses, subscriptions, notes and study tools (the “Service”).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Service, why we collect it, how we store and protect it, and the choices and rights you have over your data. By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

02

Information we collect

Account & profile information

  • Google sign-in: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account.
  • Email sign-up: your name, email address, an optional mobile number, and a password. Passwords are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your plain-text password.

Purchases & subscription records

When you buy a course or subscribe, we keep records of your order and plan details — the items purchased, price paid, billing cycle, purchase date, coupon or referral codes used, and your subscription status and expiry date.

Learning activity

To run the Service we store your course progress, completed lessons, saved notes, bookmarks and the resources you open.

Push-notification subscription

If you enable notifications, we store a Web Push subscription token for your device so we can send you reminders and service updates. You can switch this off in your profile settings at any time.

Technical & usage data

We collect basic technical information such as device type, browser, operating system and coarse usage analytics through Firebase, to keep the Service reliable and secure.

Google Drive data

We request limited access to your Google Drive only for the personal-copy feature. This is explained in detail in the next section.

G

Google user data

Important This section describes how Eduvora uses information received from Google APIs. It is the most important part of this policy.

Eduvora requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive for one purpose only: when you tap “My copy” on a course file, we use Google Drive’s files.copy method to create a copy of the master course file in your own Google Drive, at your request. The copy is created under your own account and is owned by you.

  • We never store or transfer the content of your Drive files to our servers.
  • We never share your Drive data with any third party.
  • We never use your Drive data for advertising, analytics or marketing.
  • Apart from creating that one copy, we perform no read, write or delete operations on your Drive.
  • The Google access token is kept only in your browser (in memory and session storage) and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • We only store a small reference so your copy opens instantly next time: the source file ID and the ID of the copy we created for you (in users/{uid}/driveCopies/). The actual file contents remain in Google Drive.

This use is compliant with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

03

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you securely.
  • Provide the Service — courses, subscriptions, notes, progress tracking and your personal Google Drive copies.
  • Process orders and subscriptions and deliver what you have paid for.
  • Send service notices, renewal reminders and (if you have enabled them) notifications.
  • Respond to your support and deletion requests.
  • Improve the Service, and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.
04

Data storage & retention

Your data is stored on Google Firebase services — Cloud Firestore for your account and learning data, Firebase Authentication for credentials, and Firebase Storage for any uploaded files.

We keep your information only for as long as needed to provide the Service and to meet our legal and tax obligations. Account and learning data is retained while your account is active. If your account is closed or you request deletion, we remove or anonymise your data within a reasonable time, unless we are required by law to retain it.

05

Data sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that are strictly necessary to operate the Service:

  • Google — for sign-in and, only at your request, to create your personal Drive copy.
  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, storage and hosting.
  • Razorpay — payment processing (see Payments below).

We may also disclose information where required to comply with applicable law or to protect the rights, property or safety of Eduvora, our users or the public.

₹

Payments

Payments on Eduvora are processed by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We do not collect or store your card number, UPI details, net-banking credentials or any other payment instrument information. Razorpay processes your payment details directly and handles them under its own privacy policy and security standards.

06

Your rights & data deletion

You can review and update your name, mobile number and bio from your in-app profile at any time.

You can revoke Eduvora’s access to your Google account at any time from your Google account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access does not delete the copy already in your Drive — that file belongs to you.

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email us at the contact address below. We will action verified requests within a reasonable time, subject to any retention required by law.

07

Cookies & local storage

We use browser cookies and local/session storage to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and cache the Google access token for your current session. These are required for the Service to function. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell data derived from cookies.

08

Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will post the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
